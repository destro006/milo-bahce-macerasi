# Milo animation QA
Read MASTER_PROMPT_EN.md, PRODUCTION_CONTRACT_EN.md and ACCEPTANCE_TESTS.md. Choose one primary renderer based on actual source. Require eye lead, anticipation, planted feet, articulated limbs, reach/contact, attachment, carry, basket release and recovery. Do not accept moving a still image as final animation. Verify actual target disappears from its original location, appears in basket and commits once even on rapid taps/interruption. Inspect squirrel paws/tail and actual visible firefly body/wings. Verify tr-TR female narration, separate English voice, replay, mute and audio failure. Capture motion and audio evidence, test actual scene interactions in mobile view, and report measured results. No invented test passes.
