## 2. Product experience and art direction

## 4. Story system — 30 different episodes, 90 tasks
Reconcile `levels.json` against existing content rather than blindly replacing it. Build 30 distinct story episodes, each containing 3 meaningful exercises, organized into four regions/acts: home orchard, stream and forest, greenhouse and twilight, reunion/finale. Preserve established plot and characters; integrate the lantern and Dawn Bell only where compatible. Every episode has a reason to act, a character need, a small obstacle, 3 tangible tasks, an outcome that changes the environment, and a transition that follows from the outcome. No unrelated quiz interrupting the story. Start with a short intriguing action, not a lengthy exposition. Use natural short Turkish dialogue (one or two lines per beat); let characters speak, gesture, point and react. Write new connective dialogue where needed. Milo should talk with friends rather than delivering lectures. The child must solve the problem through action. Finish with an emotionally satisfying collaborative finale, not a scoreboard.

Each exercise must define a deterministic correct target, visible distractors, one spoken instruction, visual cue, optional hint, success animation, tangible outcome, and a retry path. Use stable entity IDs, not displayed strings for validation. Difficulty progression: early 2–3 choices, middle 3–4, later 4–6 with matching, order and recall; age-appropriate complexity, no mandatory timers. Every completed task contributes to a persistent story state (e.g., bridge opens, seed lights, basket fills, friend can move). Story can be replayed without erasing progress. Free Exploration may revisit unlocked scenes and practice items without pressure.

## 5. Make character animation the centerpiece
Do not move a still image up and down and call it a cartoon. Create actual rigged/layered assets with independently moving pelvis/spine, head, eyes, eyelids, brows, mouth, shoulders, upper/lower arms, wrist/hand, fingers when possible, thighs, shins, feet, ears/tail and clothing as appropriate. Use anticipation, staging, arcs, overlapping action, follow-through, spacing/easing, silhouette readability and expressive poses. Preserve physical contact and foot plants. Use a single locomotion controller with idle, turn, walk, stop, interaction, carry and reaction states, with additive facial/upper-body layers only where they do not conflict. Root motion OR controlled translation, never both. Bake/export named animation clips and document asset provenance. If no suitable rig is present, author/obtain licensed original rigged assets and demonstrate the vertical slice before mass production; report missing or inadequate art honestly.

For every correct selection: target highlight → Milo looks at target → turns and steps toward it → plants feet → reaches/crouches → hand contact → ownership transfer to hand socket → lifts and carries → hand arrives at basket → releases at rim → item settles inside → basket visibly updates → “Doğru cevap” → story state commits. Use equivalent sprite contact frames in 2D. Once selected, mark the item pending, reject repeated taps; on pickup remove it from its old position, never duplicate it. If skip/reduced motion is used, still show a brief understandable transfer and commit once. Handle animation cancellations with idempotent event processing and a recovery path. Do not let an animation callback alone block progress indefinitely.

Walk between scenes along a visible traversable path, with turning, parallax and camera tracking; cut only behind a natural foreground wipe or at a purposeful story beat. Match character speed to gait to prevent sliding. Squirrel: articulated spine, paws, alternating foot contact, tail drag/follow-through, curious head turns, blink, ear motion, crouch, small leap with landing compression, nut grip, climb only on actual surfaces. Lila: visible body, wings and glowing abdomen, graceful curved paths, soft non-strobing pulses and usable tap target; not a generic particle effect. Friends respond to events, not random repetitive loops. See production contract for clip and timing requirements.

Story Adventure (30 × 3), Counting Garden/Forest (30), Turkish Letter Forest (30), English Vocabulary Garden (30), Firefly Attention/Listening (30).
